## Actual scope

- Removed the sender/manager-side `Theo dõi lượt đọc` metadata block from `NotificationDetail`.
- Removed the manager-only expiry and aggregate read-data hints that were rendered inside that block.
- Preserved the recipient-side `Trạng thái của bạn` block when recipient read state exists.
- Updated the component regression to prove sender view does not expose read tracking or its supporting copy.
