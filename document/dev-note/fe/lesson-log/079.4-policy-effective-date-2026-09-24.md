## Actual scope

- The settings form defaults a new policy version to one day after the later of HCM today and the latest policy's `effectiveFrom`.
- After a successful save, the form advances the date again from the newly created version, preventing reuse of the same unique effective date.
- Date calculation uses `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` date parts and UTC calendar arithmetic to avoid browser timezone shifts.
