## Changes

- `BE/BaiTap-RS/src/main/resources/ai/timetable/proposal-system.txt`: define selected scope from `SNAPSHOT.data.assignments`, `classIds`, `validFrom`, `validTo`, and matching demands; explain that revision-wide `currentEntries` outside the assignment/date scope are context retained by the application, not proposal rows or a reason to ask about scope. Require exact preservation of `lockedEntryIds`.
- The prompt now maps natural-language slots through `SNAPSHOT.data.periods` fields `dayOfWeek`, `session`, and `periodIndex`, never by treating `periodId` as the lesson index. Existing-entry conflict claims must match the requested slot and selected assignment/date scope; unlocked in-scope entries can be changed to satisfy the request.
- A request to leave a nonexistent configured period empty is treated as already satisfied. A request to schedule into an absent period must identify that specific slot as missing.
- No calendar, UI/API, validator, or student-attendance changes.
